# Product language

- Write about Gresst as one product: never mention Legacy, the portals, migration, "sistema anterior", or "new vs old" (no "ya no", "reemplaza", "antes era").
- Say *cuenta*, *cliente*, *proveedor*; never *tenant*, *gestor* or *generador* as account kinds.
- Business language only: no enum, table, GraphQL or EF names, even when the source is `../API/docs/` (DOMAIN, WASTE-CLASSIFICATION, EXCHANGES, ADRs).
